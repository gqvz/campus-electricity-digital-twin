IITR Pulse — geographic campus sources

Retrieved 7 October 2026. Plan outlines and placement are geographic; architectural elevations are schematic, not surveyed.

Coverage and provenance

The shared campus contains 231 mapped building features, 229 road/path ways, 47 landscape/sport areas, and 17 individually mapped trees. Twelve buildings have illustrative audit observations. All other buildings are context, labelled Not audited when selected.

- Campus boundary: https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/52435788 (version 26).
- Source map extract: https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map.json?bbox=77.888,29.859,77.904,29.874
- Supplementary complete relation, checked and excluded as outside campus: https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/relation/6132618/full.json
- Public manifest, boundary and counts: /campus/manifest.json
- Building rings and source IDs/versions: /campus/buildings-0.json through /campus/buildings-4.json
- Other geographic layers: /campus/roads.json, /campus/surfaces.json, /campus/trees.json

The above JSON files constitute the publicly downloadable extracted database. The older /building-footprints.json is preserved as a legacy twelve-building extract; it is not the current rendering source.

The importer retains buildings whose outer-ring vertex-average lies within the OSM campus boundary and whose ring has a vertex inside it. Multipolygon member ways are not also rendered as duplicate buildings. All retained features have complete rings; the manifest reports zero incomplete-building diagnostics. This is OSM coverage, not a claim to inventory every physical building. Unmapped structures, source errors, extensions and other IITR sites may be absent. Separate features can describe blocks of one complex. Many residences/support buildings are unnamed; names are not invented. OSM boundary data is not an official surveyed administrative boundary.

Coordinate and height contract

Source coordinates remain WGS84 longitude/latitude. A reversible local tangent-plane approximation uses origin [77.895, 29.865], x=east, y=south, 111320 metres/latitude degree and a cosine longitude scale. Every building and mapped layer uses the same scale: 50 metres per scene unit. No individual footprint is resized, translated to a grid cell or rotated relative to its source. Courtyards, disconnected volumes, spacing, orientation and relative widths are retained. The approximation is for campus visualization, not engineering measurement.

Elevation uses OSM height where available, otherwise OSM levels × 3.6 m, otherwise assumed levels × 3.6 m. Unknown context defaults to one assumed level. Evidence panels identify the height basis. Floor separation adds schematic display gaps; groups are not surveyed interiors. Road centerlines are sourced, but untagged widths are visual estimates. Only individually mapped trees are drawn, not a complete vegetation survey.

The selected Chromatic interface uses this dataset. Focus, zoom, rotation and pan transform the whole camera view; they do not relocate buildings. The accessible searchable HTML list exposes all 231 features. Alternative designs and the original prototype route have been retired.

Electricity data is explicitly fictitious design-review fixtures. Geographic placement does not validate observations, and context buildings do not acquire fabricated wattage or kWh.
