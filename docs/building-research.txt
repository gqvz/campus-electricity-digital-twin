# Building appearance research

How the 3D campus buildings were derived. The plan outline of every building is
the sourced OSM footprint. Everything vertical is a **schematic study** drawn
from photographs, not a survey, and the app labels heights accordingly
("Photo-based estimate").

## Footprint review against satellite imagery

OSM often traces a hostel or department *complex* as one filled outline even
when the ground shows separate bars around lawns (Ravindra Bhawan is five bars
and two wings, not a block). Satellite tiles of the campus were compared with
the OSM outlines and the coarse ones were corrected:

| Building | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Ravindra Bhawan | OSM envelope replaced by the five E-W bars and two N-S wings; the enclosed ground is drawn as lawn. |
| Govind Bhawan | Bars and wings traced; one large central lawn. |
| Computer Science & Electronics (`ece`) | U-shaped block around a lawn instead of one slab. |
| Mechanical Department | Scattered blocks instead of a 134 m x 135 m envelope over open ground. |
| Management Studies | Central courtyard opened. |

How: `tools/refine_footprints.py` converts shapes read from georeferenced
screenshots (`tools/footprint-refinements.json`, pixel coordinates plus the
tile's centre and metres-per-pixel) into rings in
`src/designs/campus/refinements.json`. The original OSM rings stay in the
source data, and refinements may extend at most a few metres beyond them.
Pixels were read by eye, so corrections are approximate (about +/-2 m at
0.31 m/px, +/-4 m at 0.62 m/px). No imagery or traced geometry is stored.
Many other outlines (north hostels, most departments) already matched the
imagery and were left alone; a few unmapped buildings are absent from OSM and
therefore from the model.

## How the model uses it

- `src/designs/campus3d/styles.ts`: the seven parametric vocabularies
  (colonial, sandstone, grid, hostel, sandblock, concrete, plain) and which
  building takes which.
- `src/designs/campus3d/buildingModel.ts`: wall, roof, window, balcony, column,
  portico, dome and tower generators. Thomason anchor points (dome, portico,
  tower) are fixed in campus metres and unit-tested to sit on solid footprint,
  outside the courtyard.
- `src/designs/campus3d/terrain.ts`: ground, surfaces, roads and vegetation.

## Known limits

- Storey counts, bay spacing and window sizes are typical-for-style values.
  Only Thomason (12.6 m) and the library (17 m, four levels) have photo-based
  heights; everything else keeps the OSM height/levels tag or the earlier
  assumption.
- The library's roof dome, and the tower position behind the Thomason dome,
  are read from low-resolution photos and should be confirmed on campus.
- Context buildings use the style chosen by name/kind, so a department-style
  facade on an unnamed block is a guess, not an observation.
- Trees: the 17 mapped OSM trees are exact; additional planting inside mapped
  gardens/parks and along main roads is illustrative.
- MAC, SAC and LHC detailing (roof frames, diagonal screens, vertical louvers)
  carries over the earlier SVG study and official-photo notes.
